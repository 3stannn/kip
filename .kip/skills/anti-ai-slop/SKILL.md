# Anti Ai Slop

Use concrete language tied to the product and user action. Remove marketing filler, redundant summaries, invented testimonials, fabricated numbers, decorative badges, and repeated card grids without a content reason. Preserve intentional style and required detail. In code, prefer direct names and existing conventions; avoid unnecessary wrapper layers, speculative abstractions, and comments that restate syntax. Do not equate brevity with correctness.
